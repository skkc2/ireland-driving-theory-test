# Question bank audit — 7 November 2026

## Finding
The bank is **not validated**. Do not describe it as 600 validated or 600 independently written questions.

A structural review of `data/questions.json` found 600 records across 12 topics, but the questions were generated by cycling a much smaller set of scenario facts and changing the prompt wrapper. Normalising those wrappers gives 22 distinct scenario stems per topic: **264 distinct stems in total and 336 repeated scenario rows**. Different IDs and superficial prompt wording do not make repeated underlying scenarios independent questions.

The existing options and explanations are not individually sourced. A structural check can detect missing fields and duplicated stems, but it cannot prove that a legal answer is correct. No independent Irish instructor review has been performed.

## Safety action
- Every existing record is marked `validationStatus: "unverified"`.
- The bank metadata is marked `contentStatus: "UNVERIFIED"`.
- Mock exams and topic quizzes are paused in the website UI.
- Do not rely on the draft questions or notes as authoritative exam preparation.
- The 600-row count is retained for audit traceability, not as a quality claim.

## Official facts verified from current public sources
1. The official RSA professional-driver page says the individual C and D theory tests contain 100 multiple-choice questions and require 74 correct; the combined C+D test contains 140 and requires 104 correct. It also says the theory test covers Rules of the Road, risk perception, eco-driving, hazard awareness and safe driving behaviour.
2. The official test provider FAQ specifies time limits: C or D = 120 minutes; combined CD = 180 minutes.
3. The RSA says the separate CPC case-study test involves three real-life situations, 15 questions per situation, with at least 5/15 in each and 28/45 overall. This is a separate assessment from the multiple-choice theory test.
4. EU Regulation 561/2006 provides the standard driving-time and break framework, but exceptions and special provisions mean simplified questions need careful qualification.
5. Irish HSA guidance confirms ADR driver training/certification requirements and exemptions are class- and operation-specific. Generic statements such as "all dangerous-goods loads require an ADR card" would be unsafe.

## Important scope correction
The RSA theory test page identifies the test categories as C, D and combined C+D. C1, C1E and CE should not be presented as separate theory-test formats without evidence; the correct truck/bus theory category must be confirmed against the current RSA/booking guidance. Dangerous goods is specialist ADR material and should be labelled supplementary unless the official test syllabus specifically confirms its inclusion. CPC case-study preparation must be a separate module if included.

## What "validated" must mean
A question can only be marked validated when its exact proposition, correct answer and explanation have been checked against a current, specific authoritative source. Each record should store a source URL plus section/page/article, check date, reviewer and any qualification/exception. Questions that cannot be grounded, or whose answer depends on unspecified facts, must be rewritten or removed.
